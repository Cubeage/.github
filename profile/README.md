## What we build

**Cubeage** is a global game studio and publisher founded in Hong Kong in 2014. We craft original mobile and web games across genres — from quick daily sessions with friends to deep competitive seasons — designed for instant pick-up and lasting play on iOS, Android, and the web.

| Focus | What players get |
|-------|------------------|
| **Instant multiplayer** | Smooth matchmaking, cross-device accounts, and live social play |
| **Live seasons & events** | Ongoing progression, ranked competitions, and community events |

> Play together, anywhere. Free to start.
